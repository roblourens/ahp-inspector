<objective>
Introduce the architectural spine of this phase: a single `selectionSource: "search" | "explicit"` discriminator on the Zustand store (no duplicate selected index, per the agent's-Discretion guidance) and route search-driven navigation through it. Gate the narrow-screen detail drawer on `selectionSource === "explicit"` so repeated Enter/Shift+Enter never opens or re-focuses the drawer, while explicit clicks still do. Keep the desktop rail syncing without stealing focus. Make Escape (while find is open) the authoritative close handler that preserves results and focuses the current matching row.

Purpose: Implements D-01..D-05 (navigation vs. opening details) and D-13 (Escape focus), which RESEARCH identifies as the single bug-class root (state conflation in `select(idx)`).
Output: store discriminator, AppShell drawer suppression, TimelineRegion search-source + Escape row-focus, with extended tests.
</objective>
